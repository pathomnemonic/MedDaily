# Reviewer guide

Thank you for helping check the medical content of **MedDaily**, a mobile arcade of daily medical mini-games for students and curious people.
The games state facts, so we would like a clinician to confirm them. This is education and entertainment, **not a clinical decision tool**, and nothing here involves real patients.

## What we are asking
Open your spreadsheet (CSV opens in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers). Each row is one item a player will see. Read **Content** and **Keyed answer**, then fill in the
**VERDICT** column:

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **OK** | Correct and fine as written |
| **FIX** | Wrong, misleading, outdated or ambiguous. Put the corrected version in *Correction or better wording* (and the source in *Source you used*) |
| **REMOVE** | Not worth keeping: unfixable, too contested, or too obscure to be useful |

Leave the verdict blank if you are unsure or the item is outside your field, and say so in *Comment*. Please add your initials in the last column.

## What to check
- The **What to check** column says what matters for that row. In general: is the keyed answer correct, is it the only reasonable answer, and are the other options clearly wrong?
- **Numbers** (reference ranges, prevalence, doses, counts): right to within a sensible tolerance for teaching? Right units? We use rounded, commonly taught values.
- **Management and order of steps:** consistent with current mainstream guidance (for example ACLS, Surviving Sepsis, NICE or specialty-society guidelines). Name the guideline and year if you correct something.
- **Hoofbeats and Deep Scan:** the percentages and rarity tiers are approximations of how common an answer is among clinicians. Flag any answer that is invalid, a missing common one, or badly mis-tiered.
- **Casefile:** is the presentation really caused by that cause, and is the finding the classic clinching test or sign?

## What not to spend time on
- Writing style, spelling of words in puzzles, or difficulty balance (unless a fact is wrong).
- Regional guideline differences that do not change the keyed answer. A note in *Comment* is plenty.
- The "Suggested specialty" column. It is only a guess to route sheets; skip rows outside your field.

## Priorities
Rows are sorted high-risk first. If your time is short, do the first rows and stop; partial sheets are still useful. About a minute an item is typical.

## Returning the sheet
Save as **CSV** (keep the column headers unchanged) and send it back to whoever sent it to you. Please do not add or reorder columns.

## Credit
Reviewers who agree will be credited by name (or anonymously, as you prefer) in the project documentation. Reviewing is advisory: the app remains an educational game and its content is not clinical advice.
