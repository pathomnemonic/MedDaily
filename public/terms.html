5. Party rooms

Party rooms let you and up to seven other people play the same puzzle and compare scores.

  • A room is identified by a six-character code. Anyone who has the code can join (until the room is full), so share it only with people you want to play with.
  • Everyone in a room can see the display names and scores of the others in that room. There is no chat or messaging between players.
  • Rooms are temporary. A room and its scores are removed automatically after it has been idle for about a day, or sooner when everyone has left.
  • Scores come from each player's own device and are not independently verified, so treat room results as friendly competition. Do not use them for prizes, grades or wagers.
  • Treat other players with respect. The rules in section 7 apply in rooms. We may remove a player, reset a display name, or end a room if it is used to harass others or break these terms. If you see abuse, tell us (section 17).
