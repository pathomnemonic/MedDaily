Help and FAQ

Clinicle: daily medical games

How do the daily puzzles work?

Every game has one new puzzle each day, the same for everyone. The day changes at midnight UTC, which is the afternoon or evening in the Americas (for example 5:00 PM in Phoenix, and 7:00 PM in New York in winter or 8:00 PM in summer). After each round the app tells you the time the next puzzles arrive on your own clock. Finish a game once and your result is saved. Free Play gives you extra rounds that do not count toward your streak.

How do streaks work?

Play at least one daily game on a day to keep your streak going. Your streak calendar and best streak are on the Trophies page. In the mobile app you can switch on an optional daily reminder in Settings; it skips days you have already played.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one and your progress stays on your device. An account only backs up your progress so you can use it on another device. Sign in from Settings with Google or an emailed code.

I lost my progress. Can I get it back?

If you were signed in with backup on, sign in again on the new device and your progress is restored. Otherwise it only exists on the device you played on. You can also export a backup file from Settings and import it elsewhere.

How do I delete my data?

Settings has "Erase data on this device", which removes everything Clinicle stored on that device, and, if you have an account, "Delete my Clinicle account", which removes your Clinicle profile and cloud progress. To have your shared PathoMnemonic sign-in removed as well, email us.

How do party rooms work?

Open Party, pick a game to make a room code and share the code or the invite link. Everyone who joins with the code plays the same puzzle and sees the scores. Rooms disappear after about a day. After a round, "Rematch" opens a fresh room for the same game.

I found a question that looks wrong

Thank you for telling us. After any round, the result card has Report a problem with this puzzle, which opens an email with the game, date and puzzle number filled in. Tell us which question or answer looks wrong and, if you can, what the correct answer is and where you checked it. You can also use "Send feedback" in Settings.

Is this medical advice?

No. Clinicle is study material and entertainment, and the cases are fictional. Never use it to make decisions about a real patient. See the terms.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the app works without a connection, and in the mobile app it is bundled. Results are saved on your device and, if you use backup, uploaded when you are back online. Party rooms need a connection.

Still stuck?

Email patho.mnemonic1@gmail.com. It helps to say what device you use and what you tapped just before the problem.
